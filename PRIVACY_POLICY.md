## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **CosmicGuide** ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and services.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide accurate Vedic astrological charts, horoscopes, and personalized insights, we may collect the following information:
- **Astrological Profile Details:** Date of birth, exact time of birth, and place/city of birth. These are strictly required for generating authentic Kundlis (birth charts) and planetary transits.
- **Account & Authentication:** If you sign in using Google Sign-In or email, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo identifier.
- **Usage & Diagnostic Analytics:** Non-personally identifiable diagnostic and telemetry data to ensure app stability and performance.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information solely for:
- Calculating planetary coordinates, dashas, nakshatras, and personalized Vedic horoscopes.
- Authenticating your account and synchronizing saved charts across your devices.
- Improving app speed, feature reliability, and user experience.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third Parties
**We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information.** Your data is shared only with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate the app:
- **Google Identity Services:** For secure authentication.
- **Secure Cloud Infrastructure:** For encrypted database storage and API processing via HTTPS.

## 5. Data Security
All network communications between the CosmicGuide mobile app and backend services are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Stored data is safeguarded with strict access controls.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion
You retain full control over your personal information. You can request the complete deletion of your account and associated astrological profiles at any time by emailing us at [support@cosmicguide.ai](mailto:support@cosmicguide.ai) with the subject "Delete My Account". All corresponding records will be permanently removed within 30 days.

## 7. Children's Privacy
CosmicGuide is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
